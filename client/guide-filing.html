Step by step: filing a problem

Six steps between an observation and an actionable case file
1

Spot the problem and gather evidence

Notice the failure where it happens: note the exact district and block, who is affected and roughly how many people, and how long it has been going on. A few clear photos or documents make everything faster later.

Who · Any citizen
2

Describe it once, precisely

Write a title that states the failure, not a solution ("Drainage channel silts up every monsoon", not "Build a wall"). Pick the closest domain category and set severity and urgency honestly — this is what matching runs on.

Who · Reporter
3

File through the report form

The Report a Problem form captures location, affected count, tags and supporting files. On submission the issue receives a permanent case number (SS/JH/2026/####) and appears on the reporter's My Problems page.

Where · Report a Problem
4

Automatic checks run first

Before a human reads it, SolveSamaj runs near-duplicate detection — repeat reports of the same issue are merged so supporters combine instead of fragmenting — and proposes a domain classification for reviewers to confirm.

Who · The platform
5

Admin review

A government administrator checks the report for completeness and genuineness, corrects categorisation if needed, and queries the reporter when information is missing. Nothing enters the public desk unreviewed.

Who · Government admin · Status · Under Review
6

Validated, backed and opened

Once validated, other citizens can add their support to strengthen the case. With community backing recorded, the file is stamped Open for Solutions and pushed into the queues of organizations whose expertise matches the domain.

Status · Validated → Open for Solutions

What makes a strong report

  • ✅ Exact location — district and block named
  • ✅ An honest estimate of people affected
  • ✅ Photos or documents attached as evidence
  • ✅ Severity & urgency filled truthfully
  • ✅ Consequence stated — what breaks if it waits

Reports with evidence pass review faster and attract more supporters.
