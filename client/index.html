HOW IT WORKS

From report to resolved impact.

Five stages, fully tracked — every case file carries its live status on the desk.

01

Identify

Citizens and local institutions submit real problems with evidence and location.

02

Validate

Duplicate checks and admin review confirm the problem is genuine and categorized.

03

Connect

The platform routes each case to universities, NGOs, startups and industry that match.

04

Collaborate & deliver

Partners co-design, prototype, field-test and deploy inside a shared workspace.

05

Measure

Outcomes are recorded on the case file and published as public proof of impact.
