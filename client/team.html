What SolveSamaj does

SolveSamaj — "solve" plus samaj, Hindi for society — is a structured bridge between people and institutions who face societal problems, and universities, NGOs, startups and industry who have the expertise to solve them. Citizens report a challenge once; the platform validates, categorizes, and routes it to organizations whose capabilities match — who can then form collaborative projects, track milestones, and record real-world impact.

The government is one possible administrator of the platform, not its only dependency — an NGO, university consortium, or CSR body could run the same system.
